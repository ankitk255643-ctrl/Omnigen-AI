# OmniGen AI Subscription Tiers

Based on the compute requirements, API costs, and feature complexity of OmniGen AI, here is a proposed breakdown for the subscription tiers: Basic , Pro, Epic, and Customised.

## 1. Basic (Free / Entry-Level)
*Pricing: ₹75/month (Max for Indian Students)*
*Target: Casual users and hobbyists who need simple, low-compute utilities.*
* **AI Assistant**: Access to the standard AI chat with rate limits.
* **Text to PDF & Word to PDF**: Simple document formatting.
* **Image Merger**: Basic image processing.
* **File Converter**: Standard format conversions.
* **Text to Script & Text to Prompt**: Text-based creative tools.

## 2. Pro (Mid-Tier Subscription)
*Pricing: ₹250/month (Max for Indian Students)*
*Target: Creators and professionals who need advanced text and image manipulation.*
* **Includes all Basic features.**
* **Gemma-4 Chat (Bytez)**: Access to more advanced local/specialized chat models.
* **Text to Image**: High-quality AI image generation.
* **Image to Text**: Vision AI analysis for images.
* **Background Remover**: AI-powered background removal.
* **PDF Editor**: Advanced PDF manipulation and editing.
* **Text to Code**: Coding assistant capabilities.
* **Video Stream**: Standard video streaming features.

## 3. Epic (Premium Subscription)
*Pricing: ₹500/month (Max for Indian Students)*
*Target: Power users, video editors, and heavy content creators requiring massive compute.*
* **Includes all Pro features.**
* **Text to Video & Image to Video**: Heavy compute video generation models.
* **Video to Text**: Analyzing and transcribing video content.
* **Text to Song**: AI audio/music generation.
* **Video Editor**: Full-featured Canva-style video editing.
* **Watermark Remover**: Advanced AI inpainting to remove watermarks.
* **Voice Command**: Hands-free AI interactions.

## 4. Customised (Enterprise / API Users)
*Target: Businesses, developers, and power users who want full control.*
* **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)**: Allow users to input their own Gemini, AIML, or Json2Video keys to bypass application rate limits.
* **Unlimited Generations**: No artificial limits (billed according to custom usage).
* **Custom Personality Engine**: Fine-tune the AI Assistant's personality for brand voice.
* **Priority Support & API Access**: Direct access to OmniGen's internal endpoints for enterprise workflows.
